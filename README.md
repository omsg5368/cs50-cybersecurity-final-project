### 📋 Non-Negotiable CS50 Compliance Checklist

Before uploading your video, ensure every item below is satisfied:

- [ ] **Slide 1 Written Items (Must appear visually on Slide 1):**
  - [ ] Your Full Name (and location, if desired)
  - [ ] Your edX Username
  - [ ] Your GitHub Username
  - [ ] Recording Date (e.g., October 8, 2026)
  - [ ] Incident Month and Year: **March 2024**
  - [ ] CVE Identifier: **CVE-2024-3094**
- [ ] **Slide 1 Spoken Items (Must be said out loud within the first 45 seconds):**
  - [ ] Introduce yourself by name, edX username, and GitHub username verbally.
  - [ ] State the date of the recording verbally.
  - [ ] State the month and year of the incident (**March 2024**) and cite **CVE-2024-3094**.
  - [ ] State why you chose this topic and how it relates to the course verbally.
- [ ] **Strict Time Limit:** Video duration must be **between 7:00 and 10:00 minutes** (e.g. 7:45, 8:20, or 9:15). Videos under 7:00 or over 10:00 will be rejected!
- [ ] **Language:** Presentation delivered completely in English.
- [ ] **Narration:** Must include verbal narration (your voice or clear automated voiceover; silent slides are not accepted).
- [ ] **Format:** Slideshow required (screencast with slides visible).
- [ ] **Target Audience:** Assumes course staff audience (lower-level technical depth included: IFUNC, GOT, dynamic linking, OpenSSL hook, Ed448).

### 🎙️ How to Record Your Presentation

#### Option A: QuickTime Player (Built-in on macOS)
1. Open [presentation.html](file:///Users/__o_m_s_g__/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/cs50_cybersecurity_project/presentation.html) in Chrome, Safari, or Firefox.
2. Fill in your name and usernames on Slide 1.
3. Open **QuickTime Player** -> **File** -> **New Screen Recording**.
4. Set microphone input to your microphone.
5. In your browser, hit **Fullscreen (`F`)**, click **Start Timer**, and start recording.
6. Follow the script in [SPEAKER_SCRIPT.md](file:///Users/__o_m_s_g__/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/cs50_cybersecurity_project/SPEAKER_SCRIPT.md).
7. Stop recording once you reach ~8 to 8.5 minutes.

#### Option B: OBS Studio / Loom
- Capture the browser window with microphone audio enabled.
